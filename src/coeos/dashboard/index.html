Theseus is a benchmark-composed router: it classifies each request into a skill axis and relays it to the model the TMB benchmarks prove best on that axis — one call, cheaper and faster than multi-model fusion. This box runs on your machine with your own keys: nothing leaves it but the call to your provider. The routing table comes from a public settings repo and is kept fresh; you always call a single model, CoeOS.
